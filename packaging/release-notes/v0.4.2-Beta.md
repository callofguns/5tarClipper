## 5tarClipper v0.4.2-Beta: a new name, and sharper pictures on the website

GTA VI Clipper is now called **5tarClipper**. Everything works the same, and your settings, downloaded tools and clips come with you.

### What's new

- **A new name: 5tarClipper.** You'll see it on the window, in the Start menu, on the desktop shortcut and in the installer.
- **Your things move with you.** The first time you open 5tarClipper, your settings, downloaded tools (FFmpeg, the YouTube helper, the GPU files) and saved work move to the new 5tarClipper folder. Your `Videos\GTA6 Clipper` folder becomes `Videos\5tarClipper`. Nothing needs downloading again.
- **Old leftovers cleaned up.** The installer removes the old "GTA VI Clipper" program folder and shortcuts.

### What's fixed

- **Tidier clip details.** Under each clip, the times, length and score now fit on one line, like "4:13–4:39 · 27s · 9/10", with **Download** on the right. Before, they wrapped untidily when several clips sat side by side.
- **Easier to see what's selected.** In the dark look, the chosen option in switches like "Paste link / Upload file" is now lighter than the rest, so it stands out.
- **Clearer message when Ollama is closed.** If Ollama is installed but not running, Setup now tells you to open it, instead of asking you to install it again.

### On the website

- **Sharp screenshots.** New screenshots taken at double resolution, so they look crisp on every screen. They also keep their real shape now; before, two of them were stretched.
- **The new name** throughout, and the Download button now gets `5tarClipper-Setup.exe`.
- **Better on phones.** The big headline wraps naturally on small screens.

### Updating

If you have v0.4-Beta or v0.4.1-Beta, open the app and click **Update now** in the banner at the top. It installs and reopens as 5tarClipper. On v0.3-Beta or older? Download **5tarClipper-Setup.exe** below and run it once; after that, updates take one click.

### Files

- `5tarClipper-Setup.exe`: the installer. This is the one you want. Windows may say the app is from an unknown publisher because it isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- `5tarClipper-Setup.exe.sha256`: used by the app's updater to check the download isn't damaged. You don't need to download this.
- `GTA6-Clipper-Setup.exe` and its `.sha256`: the same installer under the app's old name, so versions from before the rename can update themselves. You don't need these.

Only clip videos you have permission to use.
