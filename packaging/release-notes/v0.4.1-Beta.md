## GTA VI Clipper v0.4.1-Beta: a cleaner new look

A design update: the same features, easier to find and easier to read. It's also the first update to arrive through the app's one-click updates.

### What's new

- **A sidebar for your videos.** **New video** and the list of your videos now sit on the left, and the New video form opens in the main area. Before, the form pushed the list off the screen once you had a few videos.
- **The sunset line.** Under each video, a thin line fills with the GTA VI pink-to-orange sunset as the video moves from Download to Render, so you can see at a glance how far along it is.
- **Light and dark look.** The app now follows the Windows light or dark setting: "Vice night" in dark mode, "Vice day" in light mode.
- **New lettering.** Text is set in Inter, a clean typeface in the same spirit as Apple's SF Pro.
- **Calmer colours.** Pink is now only used for the main button and the video you've selected. Teal means done, and red means something needs your attention.

### What's fixed

- **Easier-to-read Make clips button.** The white text on the pink-to-orange gradient was hard to read. It's now dark text on solid pink in the dark look, and white on deep pink in the light look.
- **The video list works with the keyboard.** Move through your videos with Tab and open one with Enter.
- **Less movement if you prefer it.** With Windows animations turned off, the loading bar no longer slides back and forth.
- **Clearer wording**, for example "Waiting for the video ahead of it to finish".

### On the website

- **New screenshots** of the new design.
- **Easier-to-read Download button and step numbers.** Both now use dark text on the pink, instead of white text that was hard to read.
- **New lettering.** The page uses SF Pro on Apple devices and Inter everywhere else.

### Updating

If you have v0.4-Beta, open GTA VI Clipper and click **Update now** in the banner at the top. It installs and reopens by itself, then offers to turn on automatic updates. On v0.3-Beta or older? Download **GTA6-Clipper-Setup.exe** below and run it once; after that, updates take one click.

### Files

- `GTA6-Clipper-Setup.exe`: the installer. This is the one you want. Windows may say the app is from an unknown publisher because it isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- `GTA6-Clipper-Setup.exe.sha256`: used by the app's updater to check the download isn't damaged. You don't need to download this.

Only clip videos you have permission to use.
