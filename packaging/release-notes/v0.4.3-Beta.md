## 5tarClipper v0.4.3-Beta: Live mode, in test mode

The first part of Live mode. 5tarClipper can now watch Twitch channels while they're live in GTA VI and spot the moments their chat goes wild. This version only writes those moments down, so you can check it picks the right ones. Making real Twitch clips comes next.

### What's new

- **A new Live page.** Find it in the sidebar, under **New video**. Add the Twitch channels you'd like to watch and press **Start watching**. Each channel shows whether it's offline, live in another game, or live in GTA VI. For GTA VI streams it also shows how busy chat is right now compared with normal.
- **Spots when chat goes wild.** When the last 10 seconds of chat are at least 3 times busier than the last 5 minutes, with at least 5 different people chatting, it notes the moment. Reactions like LUL, KEKW, OMG, W, "CLIP IT" and "no way" count extra. One person spamming can't set it off, and chat bots are ignored. After a moment it waits 90 seconds before noting another on the same channel.
- **Moments list.** Each moment shows the time, the channel and why it was picked, like "Chat 4.7x busier than normal: 12 people, KEKW ×8". They're also saved to `clips.csv` in the logs folder; **Open log folder** takes you there.
- **Test mode only.** It never asks Twitch to make a clip yet. Every moment is marked **Would clip**.
- **Log in with a bot account.** Live mode uses a separate Twitch account for the bot. You log in by typing a short code on Twitch's own website, so your password never goes through 5tarClipper. The login renews itself. If it goes unused for 30 days, Twitch asks you to log in again.
- **Safe to leave running.** If the chat connection drops, it reconnects by itself. It checks which channels are live once a minute. While Live mode is watching, closing the app asks first.

### Setting up Live mode

The Live page walks you through it. Once, it takes about 5 minutes:

1. Turn on two-factor authentication on your main Twitch account. Twitch asks for it before you can register apps.
2. Register a free app in the Twitch developer console (the Live page links to it). Choose **Public** as the client type, then paste the app's **Client ID** into the Live page.
3. Press **Log in to Twitch**. Approve the code while your browser is signed in as your bot account.

### Updating

If you have v0.4.2-Beta, open 5tarClipper and click **Update now** in the banner at the top. It installs and reopens by itself. If automatic updates are on, it happens when you close the app. On an older version? Download **5tarClipper-Setup.exe** below and run it once. After that, updates take one click.

### Files

- `5tarClipper-Setup.exe`: the installer. This is the one you want. Windows may say the app is from an unknown publisher because it isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- `5tarClipper-Setup.exe.sha256`: the app's updater uses this to check the download isn't damaged. You don't need to download it.
