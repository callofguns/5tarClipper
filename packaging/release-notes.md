## Download

**[GTA6-Clipper-Setup.exe](https://github.com/callofguns/gta6-clipper/releases/latest/download/GTA6-Clipper-Setup.exe)** for Windows (64-bit). No admin rights needed.

On first launch, the app opens in your browser and shows a **Setup** checklist. Use it to download FFmpeg, a YouTube helper and, optionally, NVIDIA GPU files for faster transcription. You also need [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/quickstart) installed and logged in.

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Click **More info → Run anyway**. The `.sha256` file lets you check the download.

## What it does

- Paste a YouTube or Twitch VOD link, or upload a video.
- Finds loud moments, transcribes speech, and asks Claude for the best 20–60 s moments.
- Renders 1080x1920 clips with captions, using either a center crop or a facecam-on-top layout.
- Saves clips to `Videos\GTA6 Clipper` for you to review. Nothing is ever posted.

Only clip videos you have permission to use.
